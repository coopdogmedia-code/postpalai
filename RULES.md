\# PostPal Repo Rules (non-negotiable)



\- Shared components live under /components.

\- Shared utilities live under /lib.

\- Routes live only under /app (App Router). Do NOT create /pages.

\- Keep app/layout.tsx as the single root layout wrapper.

\- Prefer small, composable components. No giant pages.

\- Do not change package manager or lockfiles.

\- Do not add new frameworks without explicit approval.
